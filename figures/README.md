This folder contains all the visualization images in the REPO. Aside from baseline, each folder contains the images to make a single image in the paper (some will contain multiple alternative possibilities for the image)

+ `assortativity_grids\` These illustrate capy and moran scores of grids with high, medium, and low assortativity. They include both visualizations of individual grids, and histograms plotting metric values against random samples of a certain type of grid.
+ `baseline/` This contains all plots made using the metro area data produced in capy-core
+ `choropleths_by_buffer\` Plots showing chloropleths colored by rho for black population clusters (Austin, the South Side, Chester, Germantown). Column shows the chloropleth for a given decade, rows denot how large a radius of neighbouring tracts are added to the cluster
+ `metrics_heatmap_by_buffers\` Shows chloropleths colored by rho by decade for Chicago and Philadelphia with clusters highlighted. For each decade it also shows a heatmaps colored Capy, Moran, Dissimilarity and Spread with buffer radius on the y axis and decade on the x.
+ `idealized_grids\` Reproductions of the plots found in figure 4 of the preprint. Includes lineplots of capy versus rho on idealized configurations on asymptotic grids, lineplots of capy versus rho on the deterministic checkerboard and constant configurations for 90x90 polygonal checkerboards, scatterplots of capy versus rho on samples of random isolated, one cluster, and multiple cluster configurations on 90x90 polygonal checkerboards, and visuals of clustered, multiple clustered, isolated, and checkerboard configurations on a 90x90 polygonal gid.
+ `idealized_grids_diffusion\` These examine stepwise diffusion of xpopulation on 10x10 grids for five initial configurations. For each configuration there are two plots: 1, a timelapse showing the polygonal grid after each step of diffusion 2, lineplots of moran, capy and dissimilarity by step.
+ `misc-observed_diffusion_figs\` Miscellaneous figures for analyzing observed diffusion. These are old, exploratory and not going in the paper.
